Mechanical design

Wheelchair-Mounted Exercise Retrofit Kit

Project manager · Core mechanical design · Senior capstone · August 2025–May 2026

A removable resistance-training system for manual wheelchairs, developed around accessible exercise, quick installation, and a compact mechanical package.

3–60 lbfAdjustable resistance
<5 lbDevice mass
<$150Production cost
<5 minValidated setup time

The challenge

Integrate adjustable resistance and reliable cable return into a removable device that fits manual wheelchairs. The mounting interface, braking mechanism, and retraction system needed to work together while keeping fabrication practical and installation quick.

My contribution

Developed the core mechanism and full SolidWorks assembly, integrating the mounting, resistance, and retraction subsystems. Led design, fabrication, assembly, and iterative testing using commercial components and custom parts.

Engineering approach

Follow the load path

Cable pull engages the spool and clutch, the brake creates resistance, and the spring returns the cable to the starting position. This sequence made alignment and structural support central to the design.

Design the wheelchair interface

Adjustable mounting features and revised hook geometry addressed inconsistent fit across wheelchairs while supporting quick installation and removal.

Make resistance repeatable

Before use brake handle calibration addressed variation between installations and supported accurate, repeatable resistance setup.

Design iteration

Clutch deflection and bearing instability

Revision: Shortened and reinforced the clutch-engagement mechanism and added an additional bearing to support axial loading.

Result: More stable load path and improved clutch/rotor alignment.

Limited compatibility and inconsistent fit

Revision: Revised hook geometry and added adjustable mounting features.

Result: Broader compatibility with quick installation and removal.

Resistance settings varied between installations

Revision: Added before usage handle calibration.

Result: More repeatable resistance setup between uses.

Outcome

The prototype combined braking, retraction, and mounting into a portable exercise device. Static, cyclic, and load testing informed revisions to critical components and interfaces.
