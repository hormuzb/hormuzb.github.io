Inverted Pendulum

Independent project · Modeling through hardware · June 2025–April 2026

A cart–pendulum platform connecting analytical dynamics, controller simulation, encoder feedback, and physical tuning.

Model → hardwareEnd-to-end development
Encoder feedbackReal-time sensing
DC motorCart actuation

The challenge

Translate the unstable upright behavior of a pendulum into a physical system that can be sensed and controlled. Success depended on the interaction between the model, sensor feedback, motor response, and mechanical assembly.

My contribution

Derived equations of motion, simulated system dynamics in MATLAB, designed and fabricated the cart–pendulum system, and created closed-loop control with Arduino, DC motor, and rotary encoder.

Engineering approach

Model the equilibrium

Developed nonlinear dynamics and linearized the model about upright equilibrium to evaluate controller behavior in MATLAB.

Close the loop

Connected encoder-based feedback to Arduino control and DC motor actuation, bridging the simulation and hardware environments.

Tune the physical system

Developed and debugged PID algorithms in MATLAB and Arduino C++, iteratively diagnosing sensor feedback, software, motor response, and mechanical behavior.

Project gallery

Outcome

Built a functional mechatronics test platform and connected analytical modeling, simulated disturbance response, and real-time implementation. Hardware tuning worked toward pendulum stabilization.
