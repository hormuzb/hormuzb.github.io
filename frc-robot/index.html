FRC Robot

Full-system mechanical integration lead · FIRST Robotics Competition

A competition robot integrating drivetrain, intake, shooter, and climbing mechanisms inside a constrained frame.

4 subsystemsMechanical interfaces
CAD → competitionDesign and integration

The challenge

Coordinate multiple mechanisms within a constrained robot frame while accommodating motors, sensors, pneumatics, and wiring. Individual subsystem designs needed to become one assembled, testable machine.

My contribution

Led full-system assembly and coordinated the mechanical interfaces between the drivetrain, intake, shooter, and climbing mechanisms. Worked with electrical and programming teams through fabrication, assembly, and controls integration.

Engineering approach

Resolve the interfaces

Coordinated drivetrain and mechanism placement in the full CAD assembly, with attention to subsystem mounting and packaging.

Build across disciplines

Worked with electrical and programming teammates to incorporate actuation, feedback, pneumatics, and wiring within the robot.

Iterate as a system

Assembled, tested, and iterated the integrated mechanical, electrical, and control systems for competition use.

Project gallery

Outcome

Delivered an integrated competition robot through CAD, fabrication, assembly, and testing. The project demonstrates full-system ownership and collaboration across mechanical, electrical, and programming disciplines.
