03 / About me

About me

I’m a mechanical engineer interested in the point where mechanisms, electronics, and control systems come together.

I earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Texas A&M and am pursuing an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon. My work spans accessible exercise hardware, independent controls projects, production tooling, and competition robotics.

At CMU’s Musculoskeletal Biomechanics Lab, I work on upper-extremity tracking using inertial measurement units and optical motion capture.
