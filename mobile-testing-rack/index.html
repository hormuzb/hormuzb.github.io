Mobile Programming & Testing Rack

Engineering intern · Full mechanical ownership · Eaton · May–August 2024

A mobile, serviceable rack designed to organize approximately $70,000 of production test equipment and support in-shop programming and equipment setup.

$70kEquipment supported
Concept → buildMechanical ownership
Factory deployedProduction testing

The challenge

Create a secure, organized equipment platform that could move around the shop and support programming and testing before on-site installation. The structure needed to accommodate equipment, mechanical assembly, and electrical integration.

My contribution

Developed the rack from initial concept through CAD, in-house tubing fabrication, and complete mechanical assembly. Drilled and assembled the structure manually before electrical integration and factory-floor deployment.

Engineering approach

Package the equipment

Used CAD to define the rack structure and equipment layout around secure organization, mobility, and serviceability.

Build in-house

Translated the model into a fabricated tubing structure and assembled the mechanical hardware using available shop processes.

Integrate and deploy

Delivered the mechanical assembly for electrical integration and deployment in production validation workflows.

Project gallery

Outcome

Delivered a finished, electrically integrated production rack. It enabled in-shop code deployment and equipment setup, helping reduce work required at installation sites.
